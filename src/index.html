WELCOME TO THE WORKSHOP

From your space to your cockpit.

  1. Set up your room

    A new project starts empty. Enter the inside width and depth, in cm. The ceiling height is an editable reference. If you already have a saved project, you go straight to the editor or back to your setup step.

  2. Place openings and furniture

    Add doors and windows, choose their wall and set their size and swing. Then place the bed, wardrobes and other furniture. You can drag them in the plan or type their positions. Walls AB, BC, CD and DA run clockwise. The orange area is a door's swing; the blue band keeps access to a window clear.

  3. Add your desk

    Enter its size and place it. Turn on only the accessories you use: monitor and arm, keyboard, PC, mouse and desk mat. You can also go on without a desk. Items on the desktop keep their size when you resize it.

  4. Design the cockpit

    Choose GT, flight, hybrid or smallest footprint. Adjust the chassis and posture and arrange the cockpit in the remaining space. In Aluminium base, the lift raises the seat, wheel, pedals and controls together without changing your posture. The desk and the monitor keep their height. Heights are measured from the floor and include that lift; seat, pedal and control positions are measured from the rear end of the chassis. Arrange turns on dragging in the plan. In Desk, with the monitor and arm on, you can set or drag the ELBOW and VESA joints.

  5. Carry on whenever you like

    You can go back to the steps you visited without losing anything, or leave openings and furniture for later. Progress and half-filled forms are saved. When you finish, the editor opens with the Room, Furniture, Desk and Cockpit sections. Export a JSON file to keep a copy or move the project to another device; the CSV and the summary only include the items you added.

What is checked

A two-segment body model for legs and arms, with average proportions from your height. The ranges are design guides you can adjust in practice, not a clinical assessment. Interference is checked approximately with oriented bounding volumes, along with the horizontal reach and the monitor height. The arm keeps the length of its two segments and lets the base, elbow and VESA mount turn in the plan. Loads, deflection, cabling, manufacturer-specific stops, the arm's full sweep and the strength of the mounts are not simulated. In the room, furniture envelopes and door swings are checked; windows use a configurable access band. Escape routes, continuous walkways, window sashes, drawers and wardrobe doors are not checked.

The desktop can be 120–200 cm wide and 60–80 cm deep, 2.5 cm thick, on the E7 Pro frame with a 63.5–128.5 cm travel without the desktop. Official Flexispot reference (Spanish) ↗.
